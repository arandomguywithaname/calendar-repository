# Aura Realms RNG

A static browser game: roll for auras, earn coins, buy potions, build an ability
loadout, and fight bots or a friend.

- **858 auras in 11 ranks**, from Common (1 in 2) up to Fury of Darkness (1 in
  1,000,000,000), then twenty more Forbidden auras beyond it (Abyssal Crown, Void Sovereign,
  Shattered Infinity, Crimson Apocalypse, Nightmare Engine, Bleeding Cosmos, Forsaken Genesis,
  Wrath of the Unmade, Eternal Damnation, Final Silence, Obsidian Requiem, Hellfire Monarch,
  Ruined Eternity, Doomsday Pulse, Cursed Singularity, Phantom Armageddon, Drowned Leviathan,
  Endless Torment, Chaos Incarnate and Null Dominion: 1 in 1.25 billion up to 1 in 80
  billion). **Administrative** has 42 auras with hand-set power: Root
  Access (1 in 500 billion, 10,000 power), Absolute Authority (1 in 1 trillion, 12,000
  power), 40 more from Kernel Panic (1 in 1.2 trillion, 12,500 power) to Eternal Operator
  (1 in 840 trillion, 32,000 power). The last rank, **Chosen**, starts with **The Chosen
  One**: 1 in 1,000,000,000,000,000 (a quadrillion), 50,000 power, its own named moves
  (Chosen Strike, Prophecy Storm, Fated Aegis, Second Coming, Final Judgement and 🌟
  **Destiny Unbound**, exactly 1,000,000 damage that nothing can block, shield, reflect
  or boost, 4 turn cooldown) plus a 7th, its own 🫰 **Snap** for exactly 1,000,000
  damage with a 7 turn cooldown, a gold orb, banner and title, and a constellation-and-god-
  rays reveal, the longest in the game. Ten more Chosen auras follow it, rarer still (The
  Awakened, Herald of Fate, The Ascended, Keeper of Prophecy, The Eternal Chosen, Avatar of
  Creation, The First Light, Hand of Destiny, The Immortal Oath and The Final Chosen: 1 in
  1.5 quadrillion up to 1 in 400 quadrillion, 56,000–135,000 power). Rarest of all is
  **The Unknown** (1 in 1,000,000,000,000,000,000 — a quintillion — 500,000 power), whose
  signature 🕳️ **Oblivion** and 🫰 **Erasure** each erase the enemy for exactly
  **10,000,000** damage that nothing can block, shield, reflect or boost. Administrative
  auras have a cyan orb style.
  Every aura has a multi-colour palette. Orbs get fancier with rarity: gloss, rims,
  swirling paint, orbiting rings, sparkles, light rays, colour shifting, a red storm for
  Fury of Darkness and the Forbidden rank, a cyan system glow for Administrative and
  golden sun rays for Chosen. On an Epic+ roll the stage erupts with a cinematic light
  show — a breathing halo, drifting god-rays, sharp spinning laser beams and a bright
  scanner beam sweeping all the way round — around the orb's own rotating sigil ring.
- **Every aura wears its own sigil.** On top of the orb each aura shows a unique glowing
  symbol (a star, rune, planet, zodiac or alchemy glyph) inside a slow-spinning rune-ring,
  picked deterministically from the aura's id — so an aura reads as its own crest, not a
  plain circle, everywhere it appears (roll screen, inventory, battle, gift popup).
- **Rolls land, they don't just stop.** Even a Common roll now takes a beat: the names
  decelerate like a slot machine with a rising tick, then the orb pops and the reveal
  fires a small colour burst, ring and glow. Rarer rolls build longer before their flash,
  banner and (Epic+) full cutscene.
- **Its own cutscene for every Epic+ aura.** Common, Uncommon and Rare rolls stay on
  the roll screen (Rare still gets the slot-machine slowdown, flash and banner). Each
  of the 704 auras from Epic upward takes over the screen with a cutscene no other
  aura shares: one of 54 scenes paired with one of 14 effect layers (shooting stars,
  snow, embers, rain, bubbles, searchlights, lightning, petals, orbiting motes,
  glitch bars, god rays, drifting dust, bokeh, ribbons), and every scene + effect
  pairing is used at most once, plus one of 8 title styles and 5 overlays. Scenes
  include a rarity-over-time graph, an odometer, slot reels, a terminal readout,
  shattering glass, an eclipse, a portal, a storm, meteors, falling code, an ECG
  heartbeat, a constellation, radar, a black hole, a warp tunnel, fireworks, a DNA
  helix, a racing clock, northern lights, a summoning circle, a VHS glitch, a growing
  tree, an hourglass, fortune cards, cherry blossoms, a ringed planet, a lighthouse,
  a volcano, dice, crystals, a stained-glass window, a circuit board, a neon sign, an
  underwater scene, fireflies, a solar flare, a comet, a spirograph, a pendulum wave,
  a lava lamp, jellyfish, a maze, a prism, a rocket launch, sky lanterns and a
  spinning globe. Beyond Axiom always gets the graph; Cosmic Veil and Fury of
  Darkness keep bespoke sequences.
- **Shiny forms.** 665 of the 847 auras (78.5%: 615 of the 784 generated ones, 18 of the
  20 extra Forbidden ones and 32 of the 40 later Administrative ones; Root Access, Absolute
  Authority and The Chosen One have none) have a shiny form
  fixed at ×2, ×3, ×4 or ×5, spread evenly, except Fury of Darkness, whose shiny is ×10.
  Rolling one of them has a 1-in-that-number chance of coming out shiny, so a shiny
  Fury of Darkness is 1 in 10,000,000,000 and a ×4 shiny Null Dominion is 1 in
  320,000,000,000. Shinies are separate collectibles: their own inventory
  card, a rainbow ring and sparkles on the orb, a rainbow name, more power (worked
  out from the shiny odds), coins multiplied by the same number, and a rainbow sheen
  plus chime over the cutscene. Only Epic and rarer shinies get a cutscene (their
  aura's own, with the rainbow treatment); a Common, Uncommon or Rare shiny stays on
  the roll screen with a shiny banner, rainbow burst and chime. The Index tags each
  aura's multiplier, counts shinies found and, for Epic and rarer, has a ✨ button to
  preview the shiny cutscene.
- **✨ Shiny shards — make any aura shiny.** Every bot you beat (in the Arena or the
  Tower) pays out **shiny shards** as well as coins — stronger bots give more (Omniarch
  is worth over 100 a win; the Training Dummy, 2). In the Arena's supply stand you can
  spend shards to turn one of your own auras shiny — **even auras that have no natural
  shiny form, like The Unknown.** The rarer the aura, the more shards it costs: a Common
  is 40 shards, a 1-in-a-billion aura 810, and The Unknown 3,240. A shard-made shiny is
  a real shiny — its own sparkly inventory card, ✨ Shiny name, and ×3 power and coins —
  and the new shiny copy is equipped for you. (Making an aura shiny with shards never
  changes what your *rolls* can produce; only auras with a natural shiny form roll shiny.)
- **Reveals always finish.** The timeline is worked out per aura so rarer tiers
  build longer and the name and odds always finish before anything fades. Taps
  can't skip a reveal until the name and odds are fully shown, so quick taps on
  Roll can't eat a rare roll. Cosmic and rarer reveals stay on screen until you
  tap (they close on their own after 30 seconds so Auto Roll never stalls).
  Names wrap between words and numbers shrink to fit on phones.
- **Secret potion.** The shop has a "? ? ?" card under the counter. The Elixir of the
  Million (1,000,000 coins, x1,000,000 luck for one roll) reveals itself to anyone
  holding a million coins, anyone who clicks the ✦ logo seven times, or the admin
  word `secret`.
- **🧍 Stickman fighters & avatars.** Both fighters in the arena are drawn as stick
  figures that bob, lunge and **act out each ability** when animations are on — a quick
  **jab** for a strike, an overhead **smash** for heavy/execute moves, a lean-and-**blast**
  for beam/barrage attacks, a reach-and-**pull** for drains, arms-up **cheer** for heals,
  a **brace** crouch for guard/shield, and a powering-up **charge** shake for focus and
  the snap. A hit **flashes the stickman red**, a **shield** wraps it in a glowing blue
  **bubble that pops** the moment the shield breaks (a hit bigger than what's left), and a
  defeated stickman **falls over**.
  Your stickman wears **your avatar**, which you **build yourself** in **Stats → Your
  avatar**: pick any **face** from a big emoji grid (36 of them — 🙂 😎 🥷 🤖 👽 🐲 👑 💀
  🦊 🧙 😈 🐼 🦁 🦄 🦖 and more) **and** mix it with any **body colour** — 18 swatches plus
  a full **custom colour picker**, so the combinations are yours. A live preview shows
  your stickman as you change it. Your avatar also shows next to your name in the header,
  and travels to your opponent's screen in a PvP fight. An aura-wielder's stickman keeps its spinning aura
  orb glowing above its head; a bot is a steel-grey stickman wearing the bot's icon.
- **Turn-based battles with signature abilities.** Every aura has its own kit of 6
  abilities, named after the aura (Cosmic Veil brings Meteor Cascade and Supernova
  Unveiled; Fury of Darkness brings Darkness Rend and Fury Unleashed). Absolute
  Authority gets a 7th: 🫰 **Snap**, exactly 100,000 damage that nothing can block,
  shield, reflect or boost, with a 7 turn cooldown; The Chosen One's Snap deals exactly
  1,000,000. **The Snap animation:** the screen goes dark and a drawn hand rises with
  its middle finger pressed on its thumb, trembling as the snap builds; then the finger
  slides off and slaps the palm with a crack and a star of light where they met. Then
  the whole target (picture, name and health bar) crumbles into dust from the side
  facing the snapper and blows away while its HP bar drains and the number counts
  down. If it survives it pulls itself back together; if not, it stays gone. Auras
  rarer than 1 in 3,000,000 snap with a golden gauntlet: cinema bars and six stones
  that light up one by one with energy crackling around it. HP bars now drop when a
  hit lands instead of when a move starts. Choose 4 per
  aura in the Abilities tab. 15 mechanics: strike, heavy blow, guard, heal,
  precision crit, drain, focus, poison, weaken, stun, mirror, burst, 3-hit barrage,
  execute, and absorbing shield. Rarer auras roll the nastier mechanics. Bots have
  their own named kits (Titan Bot has no healing move). 37 bots run from the Training
  Dummy (power 15) up: after Eternity Bot
  come Nova, Kraken, Sentinel Prime, Cyber Hydra, Grim, Colossus, Storm Titan, Abyss
  Lord, Solar, Lunar, Galaxy, Chrono, Quantum, Overlord and Creator (power 120,000,
  +10,000,000 coins), then seven even stronger ones — Destroyer Bot, Singularity Bot,
  Leviathan Prime, Oblivion Bot, Apocalypse Engine, The Architect and finally **Omniarch**
  (power 1,200,000, +300,000,000 coins) — a real test even for a top-rank aura. Every mechanic has its own battle animation (slashes, a
  falling hammer, lightning, a nova, a hex shield, a life-drain stream, and so on),
  with the attacker lunging or casting, a floating ability name, and a matching
  sound. When the attacker's aura is Legendary or rarer, the ability takes over the
  whole screen as its own little scene, like the snap: a giant sword forms and
  swings in one arc that cuts the screen in two (strike), a colossal hammer slams the
  ground and cracks it (heavy blow), a shield drops and unfolds into a hex wall
  (guard), wings of light unfurl (heal), a sniper scope locks on and cracks the glass
  (precision), crimson tendrils drag life back (drain), a giant eye opens (focus), a
  venom flask shatters into a skull cloud (poison), chains wrap the target over a curse
  sigil (weaken), a storm drops a forked lightning bolt (stun), a mirror spins in and
  throws back beams (mirror), an energy sphere fires a huge beam (burst), meteors rain
  down (barrage), a reaper's scythe sweeps (execute) and a hex dome builds itself
  (shield). **Every aura has its own signature** behind its moves, built from its id:
  a unique magic circle (a 3- to 9-sided polygon or star, 1 to 3 rings, its own glyph
  alphabet, core emblem), its own drifting particles (embers, petals, snow, sparks,
  bubbles, shards, ash) and backdrop (rays, nebula, starfield, aurora, rain, void
  rings or a grid), all in the aura's colours. An aura's moves share its signature,
  so two auras with the same move look clearly different while the action (sword,
  hammer...) still reads the same. The target reacts in the arena when the hit lands: it is cut in two and
  slides back together, squashed flat, zapped, knocked back, poisoned green, chained,
  drained grey and so on (bots' hits do this too). **A fight is its own clean screen:**
  starting any fight (a bot, a Tower floor or a friend in PvP) scrolls to the top and
  hides the Battle tab's heading, the PvP box and the whole bot list, so you see only
  the arena instead of the fight buried in the middle of the battle section. When it
  ends, a clear button brings you back — ⚔️ Back to the bots for an arena bot, 🗼 Back
  to the Tower for a Tower floor, ⬅️ Back for PvP.
- **Cinematic moves for auras rarer than 1 in 3,000,000.** Every move of those auras
  plays as a cinematic: cinema bars, the screen dropping to black, rays of light and
  a giant spinning rune circle in the aura's colours, speed lines rushing in, the
  move itself bigger, an impact frame (one beat of inverted colours and black speed
  lines), a big shake and boom, and embers drifting up. Destiny Unbound brings down
  a pillar of golden light on the target.
- **Kill scene.** Winning a fight with an aura 1 in 1,000,000,000 or rarer plays a
  full-screen finisher: the loser is blasted into shards and a huge word slams in over
  the aura's signature — ELIMINATED (1 in a billion+), ANNIHILATED (1 in 100 billion+)
  or ERASED (the Chosen rank). Snap kills keep their own dust finisher instead.
- **PvP with 4-digit room codes**: one player creates a room and shares the code,
  the other joins. Peer-to-peer over WebRTC via the bundled PeerJS library (uses the
  free public PeerJS signalling server). The host runs the battle; both players
  choose abilities on their turn. Rematch supported.
- **Daily gift**: claim once every 20 hours. A 7-day streak ladder gives coins plus
  potions, always including a Daily Blessing (x50 luck for one roll). Day 7 pays a
  Godly Potion, a Mystery Potion and two Blessings, and coins grow every completed
  week. Miss two days and the streak restarts.
- **59 potions** in four groups. Luck from x2 up to the Potion of Destiny
  (x10,000,000 for one roll, 100,000,000 coins), with the Rainbow Potion (x10 for 100
  rolls), Infinity Serum (x25,000) and Celestial Nectar (x100,000 for 2 rolls) on the
  way. Magnets and shiny potions: the Rarity Magnet (no Common or Uncommon), Epic
  Magnet (Epic or better), Legend Lure (Legendary or better), the Shimmer Potion (x2
  shiny chance) and the Prism Potion (every roll that can be shiny is). Coins and
  speed: x2 up to x100 coin potions (Treasure Tonic x25, Dragon's Hoard x100), Haste,
  Blitz and Time Warp (roll cooldown cut to 1/8), a Mystery Potion that turns into a
  random potion (up to the Infinity Serum) and a Mega Mystery Potion that only turns
  into potions worth 1,000 coins or more (up to Celestial Nectar). Battle: healing,
  Greater Healing and Full Restore (you can carry 3 healing potions in total; older
  saves with more keep the 3 strongest and get the rest back as coins); Strength, Titan's Brew, Rage Potion and Godslayer
  Tonic (+50% up to +400% damage); Iron Skin and Steel Wall (start with a 30% or 60%
  shield); Second Wind and Phoenix Feather (survive a killing blow once, back at 40%
  or full HP). Potions of the same kind don't stack: the strongest one counts, and a
  stronger shield or revive replaces a weaker one that is waiting. Battle potions are
  used up the moment the fight starts. Thirteen more fill out the groups: Four-Leaf Brew
  (x4 luck), Mythic Potion (x100 luck) and Supernova Elixir (x2500 luck); Glimmer (x4)
  and Radiance (x25) shiny potions; the Cosmic Magnet (Cosmic or better) and Divine Magnet
  (Divine or better); Fortune Coins (x50) and King's Ransom (x500) coin potions; the Flash
  Potion (cooldown cut to 1/16); and three battle potions — Berserk Brew (+700% damage),
  World Ender (+1400%) and the Aegis Potion (a 100%-HP shield).
- **🏟️ Arena supplies (arena-only potions).** Two potions are sold **only** at the
  Arena's own supply stand, for **doubloons**, and never show up in the Potion Shop or
  the Doubloon shop: the **🍶 Elixir Potion** (×30,000,000 luck for 2 rolls — the
  strongest luck in the game — for 60,000 doubloons) and the **🎣 Rage Bait Potion**
  (×5 damage in your next bot fight, for 46,000 doubloons). The stand also shows your
  shiny-shard and doubloon balances and holds the make-an-aura-shiny station. It sits at
  the top of the Battle tab and hides itself while a fight is on.
- **Buy amount.** A row at the top of the Potion Shop picks how many potions each Buy
  button buys: x1, x2, x5, x10, x100, x1000 or **Max** (as many as your coins cover).
  It works for **Training** too (x10 trains 10 levels, Max as many levels as you can
  afford; each level costs 35% more than the last and adds +10 power). The cards show
  the total price, the choice is remembered, and if you can't afford the whole amount
  nothing is bought (Error 435).
- **Taps never get lost.** Auto roll redraws the page after every roll; while a mouse
  button or finger is down the redraw waits until the click has gone through, so
  Buy, Train, Equip and Fight buttons always respond.
- **Use amount.** Above the potions on the Roll tab, a row picks how many potions each
  click uses: x1, x2, x5, x10, x50, x100, x1000, x10000, x100000 or Max (all you own). For luck, coin, shiny and magnet potions it combines into one longer effect
  (10x Lucky = x2 luck for 200 rolls); speed potions add up their time; Mystery Potions
  open that many at once with a summary. Potions that only keep their strongest (Strength,
  shields, revives) still use just one.
- **Collector's potion.** A locked 🔒 "? ? ?" card in the Luck potions shows how many
  of the 37 shop potions you have bought (and the icons of the ones still to buy).
  Buy each of them at least once (Celestial Nectar, Potion of Destiny, the Elixir
  of the Million and the thirteen newer potions don't count, and potions from mystery
  potions, the daily gift or the admin console aren't "bought") and ⚗️ **Potion of
  Everything** appears for
  5,000,000 coins: x50,000 luck, always shiny, Epic or better and x100 coins for 3
  rolls, Time Warp speed, and a Godslayer Tonic, Steel Wall and Phoenix Feather for
  the next bot fight. Mystery potions never turn into it.
- **Inventory** lists your auras rarest first, 48 at a time with a **Show more**
  button, and a search box that finds any aura (or its shiny) by name. It is only
  built while its tab is open, so even a collection of every aura and every shiny
  (over 1,500 cards) opens in a blink and doesn't slow auto roll down.
- **💰 Sell auras.** Every aura in the Inventory has a **Sell** button that turns one
  into coins, priced by its rarity (the square root of its odds): a 1-in-a-million
  sells for about 1,000 and a 1-in-a-quadrillion for about 31 million, and a shiny is
  worth more. Selling something valuable, or your only equipped copy, asks first; a
  sold-out equipped aura is unequipped. Stats count auras sold.
- **💰 Sell all duplicates.** A **Sell all duplicates** button at the top of the
  Inventory sells every spare copy at once — it keeps exactly one of each aura (and
  one of each shiny), so nothing you own disappears and your equipped aura is always
  safe, and turns the rest into coins. It asks once, showing the total coins and how
  many copies before anything sells. Stats count every copy sold.
- **🗼 The Tower.** A 120-floor climb on the **Tower** tab, fought with the aura you
  equip from your Inventory (its power decides how each floor goes: Easy, Fair, Hard
  or Deadly). Each floor is one bot fight using the same battle engine as the Arena,
  and when it's over a **🗼 Back to the Tower** button takes you straight back to the
  Tower (so a tower fight never dumps you on the Battle screen). The bots ramp up
  fast: floor 1 is **1,000 power** and every floor up is **×1.15** (1,000 → 1,150 →
  1,322 → …), reaching the billions near floor 120. You climb **strictly in order, one
  floor at a time** — only the next floor is fightable; a floor you've already cleared
  shows a ✓ and is locked, and floors above the next are locked too. Beating a floor
  pays **doubloons** (🪙, a separate currency from coins): floors 1–10 pay 1,200,
  floors 11–20 pay 2,300, and so on up the bands (every ten floors adds 1,100). Your
  highest floor is saved. **The Tower resets every 3 days:** every cleared floor
  re-opens, so you climb from floor 1 again and earn all those big prizes a second
  (and third…) time, while your highest-floor record is kept and the status card
  counts down to the next reset. Losing a floor costs nothing but the fight. If a bot
  is too tough, the next floor also has a **⏭️ Skip** button: pay coins (the price
  scales with that bot's power — floor power × 4) to skip it and move straight on. A
  skipped floor counts as cleared so the next unlocks, but earns **no doubloons**.
- **🪙 Doubloon shop.** The **Doubloon shop** button on the Tower spends doubloons on
  potions — luck, speed, coins, magnet, strength, revive, shield and the big heals —
  at their own doubloon prices. The 3-healing-potion cap still applies, and a
  purchase you can't afford shows **Error 435: not enough doubloons**. It also sells
  the **👹 Monstrous Potion** (20,000 doubloons), a coin potion so strong it's sold
  nowhere else: it gives **x2,000 coins on your next single roll**. Because it's
  doubloon-only, it never shows up in the regular coin shop, the Mega Mystery pool, or
  the "buy every potion" collection. **Five more doubloon-only potions** are sold the
  same way — nowhere else: **🐙 Kraken's Luck** (45,000 doubloons, x5,000,000 luck for
  2 rolls), **🏴‍☠️ Pirate's Bounty** (30,000, x10,000 coins on one roll), **🌊 Tidal
  Fury** (25,000, +2,900% damage / x30 in your next bot fight), **🫧 Abyssal Shimmer**
  (28,000, x100 shiny chance for 3 rolls) and **⚓ Deep Magnet** (50,000, your next roll
  is Forbidden or better). Like the Monstrous Potion they never appear in the regular
  shop, the Mystery pool or the collection.
- **👑 VIP Shop.** The **VIP** tab is a premium shop stocked with exclusive **offers** —
  a **🚀 Super Roll** (roll 100x faster for 50 seconds, 10 Gems),
  Coin Chest (+250,000), Coin Vault (+5,000,000), Dragon's Hoard (+200,000,000), a Potion
  Pack (3 Mega Mystery Potions), a VIP Luck Vial (×1,000,000 luck for 2 rolls), the
  **🌌 Trillion Luck Potion** (×1,000,000,000,000 luck for one roll, 100 Gems), a **VIP
  Battle Pack** (World Ender + Aegis + Phoenix Feather), a **VIP Shiny Pack** (Prism +
  Radiance), a Shiny Shard Pack (+500 shards) and a Doubloon Pack (+20,000) — all bought
  with **💎 Gems**. **Gems are a premium currency you earn free by playing, never with
  real money:** claim **25 free Gems every 20 hours**, and win **3 Gems every time you
  beat a boss bot** in the Arena.
  - **⚒️ Craft Gems.** You can also melt spare auras into Gems: **1 Cosmic aura + 100
    Common auras + 500,000 coins → 20 💎 Gems**. The weakest auras are used first and
    shinies are never melted, so your best collection is safe.
  - There is a **💳 "Buy Gems with real money"** button, but real-money payments are
    **turned off on purpose** — Aura Realms is a free game for kids, so tapping it only
    shows an honest **Error 747** (payments disabled, no processor connected) and points
    you back to earning Gems. Nothing ever asks for a card or takes any money.
- **🎁 Gift auras.** Every aura in the Inventory has a **Gift** button. Enter a
  friend's 7-digit ID and how many to send. The game looks the friend up and asks
  "Send … to Bob?" before anything leaves your inventory. Gifts wait in the friend's
  gift box on the account server and show up in their game within about 20 seconds
  while they play (even while auto-rolling), or the next time they log in, with a
  message saying who sent them (by name; the sender's ID stays private). Both players need an online account; a gift can't
  be sent to yourself or taken back, and a gift box holds up to 100 gifts until it
  is collected. Gifting an equipped aura unequips it. If the server refuses or can't
  be reached, the aura stays with you. Each gift is added exactly once, even if the
  friend plays on two devices. Stats count auras sent and received.
- **🎁 Gift coins.** A **Gift coins** button at the top of the Inventory sends coins
  to a friend the same way (1 to 1,000,000,000,000,000 per gift). The coins leave
  your account, land in the friend's gift box, and arrive with a message saying who
  sent them. If the server refuses or can't be reached, the coins stay with you.
- **💬 Global chat.** The Chat tab talks with everyone playing on the same website
  (it names the site; a different site has its own chat). It checks for new
  messages every 3 seconds while open (a red badge on the tab counts unread ones
  otherwise). A green line says it is connected and names everyone in the chat right
  now. Log in to write; guests can read. A message shows up straight away as
  "sending…", then as sent, or stays with the reason it failed and a Try again
  button. Messages show the player's name and
  equipped aura but never their ID (an ID logs into the account), are up to 200
  characters, one every 1.5 seconds; the chat keeps the newest 100. **Swearing is not
  allowed:** a message with a swear word is blocked (never posted) and, as a
  punishment, **all of that player's auras are locked for 2 days** — the worst words
  (slurs and "mothertrucker") lock them for 7 days. While locked, the Inventory shows
  a lock card instead of auras and the player can't roll, equip, sell, gift, battle or
  climb the Tower until the timer runs out; then everything comes back. The lock lives
  on the account server, so it follows the player across devices and reloads. Its own
  red errors: **981** you can't chat because of a WiFi (internet) error, **523** a code
  error (the chat server failed or isn't set up on the site, or something broke in the
  chat code), **108** an admin muted you (it says for how long), **451** you swore, so
  your message was blocked and your auras are locked (it says for how long). Admins get
  🔇 (mute 10 minutes) and 🗑️ (delete) buttons
  next to every message, and a Chat section in the admin panel to mute a player for
  any number of minutes (0 = until unmuted), unmute, see who is muted and clear the
  whole chat.
- **🏆 All-Stars leaderboard.** The Leaderboard tab ranks **every online account** by the
  **rarest aura they've ever found** (ties broken by coins, then rolls), showing the top
  100 with 🥇🥈🥉 for the top three, each player's name, their rarest aura (name, tier
  colour and odds) and their coins. If you're logged in, **your own row is highlighted**
  with a "you" tag; if you're ranked below the top 100, your row and rank are shown at
  the bottom so you always see yourself. Guests can read the board and are invited to log
  in to join. It is served by the account server (the client sends no player IDs and the
  server never returns any — a 7-digit ID is a login secret), the client caches it for 15
  seconds, and a 🔄 Refresh button reloads it. The rarity used for ranking rides along in
  each player's save (`stats.bestRarity`), so the server can rank without the aura table;
  saves from before the leaderboard backfill it from the rarest aura on their next load.
- **Login and account protection**: a new player enters a name (2 to 50 characters) and
  gets a random 7-digit ID, shown at the top of the page (click it to copy). Saves upload
  a few seconds after each change and whenever the tab is hidden; an idle device quietly
  picks up progress made elsewhere. A badge next to the ID shows ☁️ Online, Saving…,
  ⚠️ Offline (keep playing; it syncs when the connection is back) or 💻 This device (the
  site has no account server, see Deploy).
  - **Your ID is a public handle, not a password.** Each account also has a secret key,
    made on your device and sent (never shown) with every save, so knowing someone's ID
    is **not** enough to read, overwrite or impersonate their account. The server turns
    away any `load`, `save`, `claim`, `chatSend` or gift-box read that doesn't carry the
    right secret (Error 403). So you can safely share your ID for gifts. **Protection never
    blocks play:** if a device's secret doesn't match (e.g. a second device), it just
    plays on locally — rolling still works — and only online syncing waits until that
    device imports the account's Save Code.
  - **Moving to a new device uses your Save Code.** Because the ID alone no longer logs
    you in on a brand-new device, export a **Save Code** (Account → Export save code) on
    your current device and import it on the new one — the code carries your ID *and*
    secret, so it logs you in and unlocks saving there. Pasting someone else's ID on a
    device that doesn't have their code just shows "log in with your Save Code".
  - **Existing accounts** (made before protection) still load by ID and quietly protect
    themselves the first time their owner saves (trust-on-first-save), so nobody is locked
    out. If a player ever is locked out (lost device), an admin can **🔓 Unlock account
    protection** for their ID so their own device can protect it fresh.
  - Export / Import save codes also still work as a plain backup; each code carries a
    fingerprint, so a hand-edited code is refused (Error 98), and codes from before
    fingerprints can only be imported with admin unlocked. A Save button sits in the header.
- **One device rolls at a time**: rolling takes a 20-second "turn" on the account from
  the server, renewed while you keep rolling, auto-rolling or watching a reveal. If
  another device (or browser tab) tries to roll meanwhile, both devices get a red
  **Error 145** warning: "You are rolling on two devices simultaneously!", and that
  rolling on both might crash your account. The second device can roll about 20
  seconds after the first one stops (straight away if it closes the tab), and it
  first loads the first device's newest progress.
- **Error codes**, shown in red: **145** two devices rolling at once, **404** network
  or account-server problem, **435** buying a potion, training or Auto Roll (not enough coins), **102** using
  a potion (none left, a stronger one already active, healing outside a fight, not your
  turn), **98** account problems (bad name or ID, no player with that ID, a bad save
  code, or progress replaced by a newer save from another device), and in the chat
  **981** WiFi error, **523** code error, **108** muted by an admin.
- **Admin** (bottom of the Stats tab, or press `` ` `` (backtick) anywhere on a keyboard):
  locked with the admin password. Unlock opens the console and the player panel below;
  Lock closes both, and admin also locks itself when you leave the page or after 5
  minutes without using it. The password is checked against a hash in `game.js`
  (PBKDF2) and in the account function (scrypt), so it isn't written anywhere in the
  code, and the password box tells browsers not to fill in a saved password. While
  admin is locked, the browser console can't change the game either: the debug hooks
  hand out copies, and aura and ability data is frozen.
- **Admin console** (your own account, after unlocking). Commands: `luck 1000 10`, `coins 5000`,
  `give Cosmic Veil`, `giveall` (one of every aura at once), `roll Fury of Darkness`,
  `give Cosmic Veil shiny`, `roll Beyond Axiom shiny`, `potion zenith 3`,
  `shards 500`, `doubloons 20000`, `makeshiny The Unknown`,
  `training 20`, `unlockauto`, `daily reset`, `clearluck`, `reset`, `help`.
  It only changes the account on the device you're using.
- **Player panel** (Stats tab, under the console, after unlocking): lists every online
  player account (auras, coins, luck, rolls). Click a player,
  then **Add coins** (a minus number takes coins away), **Add luck** (a multiplier for a
  number of rolls) or **Delete auras** (type an aura name to delete just that one, both
  forms, or leave it empty to delete all). **Delete auras on every account except this
  player** wipes everyone else's auras. **Reset everyone's luck to x1** ends every
  player's active luck boosts (and magnet and shiny potions) at once; anyone rolling right now
  switches within a few seconds without losing a roll, and everyone else's next roll is
  at x1. **💥 Detonate** resets every other player's
  account to a brand new save (all auras, coins, potions and stats) and keeps the account
  you're logged into; you have to type DETONATE to confirm. Reset players see "An admin
  reset this account" the next time their game syncs. **⛔ Ban a player** deletes every
  aura the player in the ID box owns; it asks you to re-enter the admin password and then
  type DETONATE before it does anything. **🎯 Force a player's next roll** makes the next
  roll on the player in the ID box land on an aura you pick (type its name, tick *shiny*
  for the shiny form); it's stored in their save and happens the next time they roll, once
  their game syncs. **↩️ Restore last save** puts everyone's saves
  back to exactly how they were just before the last Detonate or ban — so an accidental
  Detonate (or a ban you change your mind about) can be undone. The server snapshots each
  account before a Detonate or ban and remembers which accounts the last one touched, so
  Restore only brings back those. Players who are online get the change within
  about 20 seconds, with an "An admin updated your account" message. To change the
  password, update both hashes (`ADMIN_PW` in `game.js`, `ADMIN_SALT`/`ADMIN_HASH` in
  `netlify/functions/account.mjs`; each file shows the command). An `ADMIN_KEY`
  environment variable in Netlify, if you add one, replaces the built-in password for
  the server. A wrong password gets Error 98.

## Deploy to Netlify

No build step and no npm install.

- **With online accounts (log in on any device, one device rolls at a time):** connect
  the GitHub repo in Netlify (Add new site → Import an existing project) and set the
  **base directory** to `rng-game`. `netlify.toml` publishes the folder, and Netlify
  deploys the account function in `netlify/functions/account.mjs`, which stores
  accounts in Netlify Blobs (built into Netlify, nothing to configure). The Netlify CLI
  works too: `netlify deploy --prod` from the `rng-game` folder.
- **Drag and drop** (the zip or this folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop) publishes
  the game. Functions are meant to be deployed from Git or the CLI, so if the account
  function isn't running, IDs only work on the device that made them and logged-in
  players see a one-time Error 404 note saying online accounts aren't set up.
  Gifting auras and the chat need the account function too (the chat shows Error 523
  without it). Everything else plays normally.
- **Human check.** On the first visit each session the game shows a small self-contained
  captcha (type the wavy characters; no outside service). It is remembered for the tab
  session, so reloads and auto-updates don't re-ask, and it is skipped for automated
  browsers (`navigator.webdriver`) so the test suite runs unblocked.
- **Skip animations button.** A ⏩ button at the bottom-left turns all animations on or
  off (reveal cutscenes, battle cinematics, the snap, the kill scene, screen shake) and
  stops anything playing the moment you press it, so rolls and fights resolve instantly.
  It sits above the cutscene overlay so it works mid-animation, and the choice is saved
  (also as a "Skip all animations" box in the Stats settings).
- **⏰ Break reminder.** After **30 minutes of continuous play**, a full-screen reminder
  appears: "Take a break — you've been playing for over 30 minutes straight. Come on, go
  do what you really need to do, like your homework!" The **first** break lasts 15
  seconds and can be skipped with the button; every break **after** that lasts a full
  minute with no skip — the button stays "Please wait…" and counts down before you can go
  back. A break pauses Auto Roll. Time only counts while the tab is in front, and a real
  break away (5+ minutes with the tab hidden or closed) resets you back to the gentle
  first break. The running total is kept in `localStorage` so a quick reload can't dodge a
  break. It works for everyone — logged in or not — and needs no server.
- **✨ Break skips.** **Gifting your best aura** (the rarest one you currently own) to a
  friend earns one **Break Skip**. A break overlay shows a gold "✨ Use a Break Skip"
  button whenever you have any, and it closes **any** break at once — even a forced
  one-minute break that normally can't be skipped. Each skip is spent when used, the count
  is saved with your account, and because giving away a rare aura is a real cost it can't
  be farmed — it's a reward for being generous.
- **Players get updates by themselves.** A tab left open for days keeps running the
  old game. Every few minutes, and whenever a player comes back to the tab, the game
  asks the site for its newest page; when that has a higher Version number it shows a
  "Version N is out" banner with Update now, and updates by itself (keeping all
  progress) when the player returns to the tab while nothing is going on. The page
  itself is served with `Cache-Control: no-cache` (see `netlify.toml`). Players on a
  version from before this one need to reload once to get it.
- **Check which version is live.** The bottom of every page shows "Version N". Each
  update raises N, together with the `?v=N` on the script and style links in
  `index.html`, so browsers fetch the new files instead of reusing old ones. If the
  live site shows an older number than the newest zip, the site is still running an
  old copy: deploy the newest zip again and reload the page.

## Files

- `index.html` – markup
- `style.css` – styling and CSS animations
- `auras.js` – deterministic generator for the 784 generated auras plus the 10 extra
  Forbidden ones
- `abilities.js` – ability mechanics and the per-aura signature kit generator
- `reveals.js` – the 54 reveal scenes, 14 effect layers, the shiny sheen, title styles
  and the unique per-aura assignment
- `game.js` – game logic, battle engine, PvP, accounts, cutscene engine
- `peerjs.min.js` – PeerJS 1.5.4 (MIT) for PvP connections
- `netlify/functions/account.mjs` – the online account server: create / load accounts,
  upload saves (with revision checks so one device can't silently overwrite another),
  the one-device roll lease, and the password-locked admin tools
- `netlify/vendor/netlify-blobs.mjs` – @netlify/blobs 10.7.13 (MIT) bundled into one
  file, so the function needs no npm install
