The problem Vital solves

Apple Health is a superb diary and a terrible advisor. It holds years of sleep, heart rate, HRV and workouts, and shows you a wall of graphs — but it can't tell you whether this week was worse than last, or why you feel flat on Thursday.

It also can't send anything anywhere: Apple deliberately gives Health no cloud API, so the data never leaves the phone by itself. Vital is the bridge that carries it out — to your own server, and nowhere else.

How it works

  1. Install Vital and allow what you're comfortable with. iOS asks about each kind of data separately — sleep, heart, workouts — and you can decline any of them.
  2. Press Send once. It uploads your history, then keeps itself current: every time you open the app, plus quietly in the background. Nothing to remember.
  3. Paste one link into claude.ai. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. After that you just ask.

Things people actually ask it

  • How did I sleep this week compared to last?
  • Am I recovered enough to train hard today?
  • My HRV has been dropping — when did that start?
  • What happened to my resting heart rate after I got ill?
  • Which workouts left me most wrecked the next morning?

Vital also computes a recovery score and a training-load score, both measured against your own 42-day baseline rather than a population average — because a resting heart rate of 48 means something different for you than for anyone else.

Where your data actually goes

  • To your server, not ours. There is no company behind this holding your health records. The server is yours; we can't read it and nobody is selling anything.
  • Two links, both yours. One lets your phone write data in; the other lets your Claude read it. Nothing else on the internet can reach your data.
  • Vital never writes to Apple Health. It only reads, and only what you approved.
  • Daily summaries, not raw samples. Roughly nine numbers a day — enough for real answers, far less than a copy of everything.

What you need

  • An iPhone with Apple Health (the data you already have — nothing new to track).
  • A paid claude.ai plan, since custom connectors need one.
  • An invite: Vital is in TestFlight while we polish it, so it isn't in the App Store yet.
