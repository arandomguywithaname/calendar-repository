You play White and move first; the CPU plays Black. Click a piece then click a highlighted square to move. Full rules: castling (move the king two squares towards the rook), en passant, check, checkmate and stalemate. You can never make a move that leaves your own king in check, and the king is never captured — games end in checkmate or a draw. Draws are called for stalemate, the 50-move rule, the same position three times, and when neither side has enough pieces to mate. Drag the strength slider to pick your opponent — it looks further ahead and plays fewer weak moves as the number rises. That number is this game's own scale, not an official chess rating, but each step really is stronger. Simplified honestly: a promoting pawn always becomes a queen, you cannot choose a knight instead.
