♟️ Chess vs CPU

Your turn Moves: 0

You play White and move first; the CPU plays Black. Click a piece then click a highlighted square to move. All standard piece movement/capture and blocking rules apply, and pawns auto-promote to a queen on the last rank. Simplified honestly: castling and en passant are not implemented, and full check/checkmate detection is not implemented — instead the game ends immediately whenever a king is actually captured.
