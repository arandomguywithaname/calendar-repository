Simplified craps: place a pass line bet and roll. On the come-out roll, 7 or 11 wins instantly and 2, 3, or 12 loses instantly ("craps"). Any other total becomes "the point" — keep rolling until you hit the point again (win) or roll a 7 (lose). Pass line pays 1:1. Chips are a fake local score for fun only, not real money, and are saved in your browser.
