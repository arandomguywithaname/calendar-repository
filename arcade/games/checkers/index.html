⚫ Checkers

Your turn Moves: 0

You play red (moving down the board), CPU plays black. Click one of your pieces then click a dark square to move diagonally, or jump over an adjacent black piece to capture it. Captures are mandatory when available — if a capture chain is possible with the same piece, you must keep capturing before your turn ends. Reach the far row to king a piece (kings move/capture diagonally in any direction). Win by capturing all opponent pieces or leaving the opponent with no legal move.
