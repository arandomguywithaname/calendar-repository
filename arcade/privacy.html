Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

The short version: Claude Arc Games has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run a server that stores anything about you. Everything the games remember is saved on your own device and never leaves it — except messages you choose to send to a friend, which go straight to their device.

Who this is about

Claude Arc Games is a free collection of 100 browser games. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to buy. You never tell us your name, your email, your age or anything else, because there is nowhere to type them.

What is stored on your device

The games use your browser's local storage to remember things between visits. This lives on your device only. We cannot read it, and clearing your browser data deletes all of it.

WhatWhy
High scores and best timesSo the game can show your record
How many times you played each gameShown in your own stats panel
Difficulty and chess strength settingsSo you don't reset them every visit
Chosen language and light/dark themeSo the site looks the same next time
Your friend code and your friend listSo friends can reconnect to you
Chat messages with friendsSo the conversation is still there when you come back
Pretend chips in the card gamesThey are play money and have no real value

You can delete all of it at any time: open the admin panel and use Reset all data, or just clear your browser's site data.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, logins or passwords.
  • No advertising and no advertising networks.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies for tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of anything, because we never receive anything to sell.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • No real money. Nothing in the games can be purchased.

The Friends feature

This is the only part of the site that talks to the internet, and it only runs if you open the Friends panel. Games connect device to device, so your messages and moves are not stored on any server of ours — we do not have one.

What that involves, honestly

To let two devices find each other, the browser uses standard WebRTC helper servers run by other companies. We do not control them:

  • 0.peerjs.com — introduces the two devices to each other (PeerJS).
  • stun.l.google.com — helps a device work out its own address (Google).
  • turn.peerjs.com and openrelay.metered.ca — used only as a fallback, when a direct connection cannot be made, to pass the traffic along.

Because of how internet connections work, these servers and the friend you connect to can see your device's IP address. That is normal for any video call or online game, but it is worth knowing. The connection itself is encrypted.

A friend code is a random code like ARC-4K2P-9XQZ. It is not linked to your name or anything about you, and you can change it whenever you like.

Chat safety. Messages you send go to your friend's device, where they are saved. Nobody moderates them and we cannot see or delete them. Please do not type your full name, address, school, phone number or passwords into chat — with anyone, on any site.

Children

This site was made by a kid, for kids. It asks for no personal information at all, shows no adverts and sells nothing, so there is nothing for a child to hand over by accident. If you are a parent and you would like the chat feature switched off on your child's device, remove their friends in the Friends panel and use Reset all data in the admin panel.

Playing offline

The site can install to your home screen and keep working with no internet. Doing that stores a copy of the games on your device. It sends nothing anywhere.
